STYLE

Halloween: original spooky instrumental music can be selected under Style → Halloween. During Oct 20–31, Auto/Mix uses it on quiet days when WeBot Radio is active. Breaking severe weather overrides the holiday music and decorations.

WeBot Radio is lo-fi and country made live by the stream page (Mix plays some of each; storms always get the darker lo-fi), so it's free and safe from copyright claims. On Auto the mood follows the weather: calm when it's quiet, darker when tornado warnings are out. To use your own songs, put MP3s in public/music (name them Artist - Title.mp3) and pick My tracks (Auto also prefers them). Only use songs you have the rights to stream.

Put it on YouTube / Twitch with OBS

  1. In OBS add a Browser source. URL: , width 1920, height 1080. Connect above to get your private link.
  2. Tick Control audio via OBS so the music, chimes and WeBot's voice all go to the stream.
  3. WeBot's voice is OpenAI Alloy through Puter.js. OBS can't open Puter's sign-in window, so sign in here instead: WeBot → Puter → Sign in & link for OBS. The stream (opened with the private link above) picks it up within a few seconds. Usage counts against that Puter account (it has a free monthly allowance); if it runs out, WeBot uses the computer voice.
  4. URL options: ?state=OK focus one state · ?voice=0 · ?sound=0 · ?music=0 · ?tts=browser computer voice · ?bot=0 · ?tips=0 · ?speed=1.5 slower shots · ?tz=America/Chicago clock zone.
