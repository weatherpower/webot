Reading the map

Zoom in for NEXRAD-resolution Level 2 radar (0.25 km, straight from the radar site, under a minute old) with reflectivity, velocity, correlation coefficient and ZDR. Zoomed out, you see the national composite.

Radar colors go green (light rain) → yellow → red (heavy) → purple (hail). Warning boxes are drawn in the NWS color for that warning: red for tornado, orange for severe thunderstorm, green for flash flood. Click a box for the details.
