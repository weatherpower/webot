We check the NWS every two minutes and ping your topic once per warning. Nothing else, ever. Turn it off anytime from this page on the same phone.

Why ntfy?

Texts cost money and emails get buried. ntfy is a free, open-source notification service: you subscribe to a topic name, we post to it, your phone buzzes. We never see your number, your email or your name. Your spot and topic are the only things saved.

This is a backup, not your only line of defense. Keep Wireless Emergency Alerts on and a weather radio nearby. We can miss a warning if our server is down.
