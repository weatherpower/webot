Your photos, on the stream

Send us your storm

Hail on the porch, a shelf cloud over the pasture, the creek over the road. Send it in and WeBot shows it live, with your name on it.

Upload to any photo site and paste the link. Direct uploads are coming.

The rules

  • Your photo, taken by you. We credit the name you give.
  • Stay safe. No photo is worth driving into a storm.
  • Tell us where and roughly when.
  • By sending, you let WeatherPower show it on the stream and the site.

Reports help too

No photo? A plain report is still gold: hail size, wind damage, flooding, snow depth. WeBot reads the good ones on air. For official reports, the National Weather Service wants them too: find your local office at weather.gov.
