About WeatherPower

A passion project, built by one person who loves weather, for people who want to watch the sky without the drama.

The stream

WeatherPower Live runs 24 hours a day on YouTube. WeBot, our robot host, cycles through the top alerts, national radar, surface fronts, the SPC severe outlook, the WPC rain outlook, storm reports, the tropics, computer models and your city's forecast. When the weather gets bad, he drops everything and covers it. When it's quiet, he plays WeBot Radio and talks back to chat.

Everything on screen is real: alerts from the National Weather Service, radar from the Iowa Environmental Mesonet, outlooks from the Storm Prediction Center, the Weather Prediction Center and the National Hurricane Center, forecasts from Open-Meteo and the NWS.

WeBot

WeBot is a sassy Southern robot. He roasts what people say, never who they are, and he never cusses. His lines are hand-written, so he can't make things up on the air. He knows his weather history too: ask him about Joplin, Katrina, the Tri-State Tornado or the Enhanced Fajita Scale. Yes, fajita. It's a long story.

Not a copy

We get asked, so here it is plainly: WeatherPower is not affiliated with Ryan Hall, Y'all, and it isn't a copy of his stream or his site. We're fans of what he does. This project started as a way to learn, and it grew into something people actually watch. Different robot, different voice, different code, written from scratch and open source.

How it runs

The whole thing runs on one small cloud server for about $11 a month. The code is public on GitHub, so you can see exactly how WeBot works, or build your own.

Support

Nobody's begging. Watching, chatting and sharing the stream is support enough. If you'd like to help with the server bill, there's a "Support WeatherPower" link in the menu of the radar on weatherpower.app.
